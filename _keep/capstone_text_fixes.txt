A. STATEMENT OF THE PROBLEM  (replace the 3 prose paragraphs)
------------------------------------------------------------
The study aims to develop an Ayuda Distribution and Monitoring
Application that addresses the problems encountered in the
manual and fragmented management and distribution of government
assistance in Davao City. Specifically, the study sought to
answer the following questions:

1. How can the application enable barangay staff per district
   to manage beneficiary records in an organized database,
   given that existing records are often outdated, incomplete,
   or inconsistently maintained?

2. How can the application monitor the distribution of ayuda
   and prevent duplicate recipients per barangay, given the
   lack of effective monitoring and tracking mechanisms and
   poor coordination among implementing agencies?

3. How can the application generate reports for the efficient
   tracking and verification of the distribution process,
   supporting accurate reporting and informed decision-making?
